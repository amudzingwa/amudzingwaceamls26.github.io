reflection: |
  Looking back on the past several weeks, I am grateful for the opportunity to have participated in this program. What began as a summer research experience became much more than that. I was able to strengthen my technical skills, learn how to work effectively as part of a research team, and gain experience presenting complex ideas to different audiences.

  One of the most rewarding aspects of the program was seeing our project evolve from an initial idea into a completed research effort with a paper, presentation, and working models. There were moments of frustration when experiments did not work as expected or when deadlines felt overwhelming, but those challenges ultimately made the accomplishments feel even more meaningful.

  I also appreciated the relationships that I built throughout the summer. Whether through working with my research team, attending workshops, participating in group activities, or meeting people at the symposium, I had the chance to connect with individuals who shared similar interests and ambitions.

  As I move forward, I will carry many of the lessons from this experience with me. The program reinforced my interest in technology and research while also showing me the value of curiosity, teamwork, and continuous learning. Although the summer has come to an end, I believe the knowledge, experiences, and connections that I gained will continue to benefit me in the future.
