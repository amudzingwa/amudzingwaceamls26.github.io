what_i_learned: |
  Today we attended the AWS Summit at the Washington Convention in Washington DC. The summit had a lot of different companies, speaking engagements and sessions all focused on AWS. I persomally attended to workshops at the summit. The first sumit I went to was on how the Texas Department of Public Services automated their callcenters using artificial intelligence solutions from AWS. This session was really insightful on how AWS technologies are used in publicgovernance. I also attended a session on AI readiness for colleges and universities. The session focused on research conducted around the world on how equipped colleges and college graduates are with working with AI. The biggest takeaway for me from the session was that AI has helped college graduates transition into the workforce better than previous years, but there still is a need for institutional adoption of AI acorss US colleges. I also watched the keynote session delivered by Dave Lavey. Dave talked about the history of AWS in the past 15 years and the next frontier of innovations that they have introduced at this year's summit. The biggest one for me was the Kiro platform (which I assume has been around for a while) that is used to automate software development for companies. In conclusion, the summit was a great way to connect what we have been doing in the SAIRI institute with real life industry needs and usage of AI.
