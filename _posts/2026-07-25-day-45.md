what_i_learned: |
  Today was the first day of the NSEA Symposium. Throughout the day, I attended several presentations and had the opportunity to learn about the research being conducted by students and faculty from different institutions. It was interesting to see the variety of projects being presented and the different approaches that researchers are taking to solve important problems.

  One of the highlights of the day was the keynote speech by Juan Gilbert on Applications Quest. I found the talk to be both inspiring and insightful. One of the key lessons I took away from the presentation was how research can be transformed into real-world solutions and products that can have a meaningful impact in industry and society. The talk encouraged me to think beyond the research process itself and consider how innovative ideas can be translated into practical applications.

  I also had the opportunity to engage with stakeholders from industry as well as faculty members from other universities. These conversations were enlightening because they exposed me to different perspectives on research, career paths, and the broader impact of technology. It was valuable to hear how professionals and academics approach problems and think about the future of their respective fields.

  Today was one of the most rewarding experiences of the program so far. Attending the symposium allowed me to see the bigger picture of research and how it connects to real-world innovation. I also appreciated the opportunity to network with industry professionals and faculty members from other institutions. Those conversations gave me new perspectives and ideas that I had not considered before. Overall, the first day of the symposium was both educational and motivating, and I am looking forward to the rest of the event.
