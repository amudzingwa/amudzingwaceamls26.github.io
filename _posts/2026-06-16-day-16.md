what_i_learned: |
  Today we worked on the data development part of our project. Our main goal was to pickle and unpickle all the patches for the images we have, which amounts to over 20,000. The task was divided into four different steps, and for our team we srummed through each of steps one by one. We each had a lot of trouble with errors and error handling, which was sn important learning point but at the same time mentally exhausting. My role was building the data handler which handles the data between the pre- and post-disaster, and translating the images from GeoTIFF file into png for easier readability. Besides working on the data building, we also made initial progress on the literature review part of the paper. I personally had trouble understanding the technical aspects of thr methodology, but our graduate mentor is going to prepare a lecture on some of the technical aspects in the next few days.
  
blockers: |
  Errors and debugging. But we managed to resolve all the errors through a lot of trial and error so no bugs left for the day :)

reflection: |
  Dividing the work for the day was a great call. Although it took some time to understand the other parts that someone else was working on, we managed to make so much progress than if we had focused on the same problem as a group. I had difficulty reading the documentation for my part of the project but I asked a lot of questions and that helped a lot. I am learning to ask all the questions in my head and not feel guilty about it. I also enjoyed getting to work on the methodology section and translating what I worked on in code into literature for the paper. It is challenging but fun. All in all, I am proud of or group energy to work together and make so much progress in a day.
