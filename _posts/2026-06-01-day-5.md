reflection: |
  The lessons I took from today were from the AI quest we did. It was really nice to interact with how AI is used, and it gave me the impression of embodying the role of someone who works in AI, implementing it in the real world. Some of the important lessons I took from today were how to collect data for AI, how to train models, and how to release the model into the real world. For collecting data, I learned that you need a diverse set of data to reduce AI biases when training your model. And for training your model, you need to combine all that data and clean it up to remove identifiable information from the people you collected it from. I also learned that sometimes you need to go back and collect more data because once you train your model, you realize it needs more to be accurate. And when you release your model into the real world, you need to thoroughly test it first. Finally, I also learned that AI is not here to replace jobs but to help people do their jobs better and in a much more efficient way, especially in areas where resources are limited.
